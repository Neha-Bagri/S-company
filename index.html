Design & Operational Logic

Key functional innovations solving core challenges in commercial sensory and formulation centers.

Discreet Dual-Room Prep

The dedicated sample prep kitchen is nestled directly between the large 30-person boardroom and smaller workshop room. Technicians prepare warm, cold, or color-sensitive samples and slide them onto presentation credenzas without entering or breaking presentation flow.

20-Person Dual Sensory Testing

Combines 10 ISO-compliant individual sensory booths (color-accented panels, blind evaluation, computerized questionnaires, neutral illumination, hatch doors) with an open 10-person curved tasting bar for collaborative, unblinded client discussions.

Hospitality-Driven Science

Replacing sterile lab aesthetics with warm oak timber, fluted acoustic paneling, terrazzo counters, living green walls, and museum-grade glass cloches to create a luxury brand experience.
